Leo Augsburger: who he is and which racket he uses in 2026

Published on 30 October 2026 · by Luca Bicchieri, PadelMetrix

Leandro "Leo" Augsburger is an Argentine padel player born in Posadas (Misiones) on 11 August 2004, who plays on the left side (backhand). He is one of the strongest young players on the tour and since November 2025 has partnered Juan Lebrón. He plays with Siux and uses the Fenix Pro, a powerful diamond.

Who is Leandro Augsburger

  • Born: 11 August 2004 in Posadas (Misiones, Argentina); 1.90 m tall.
  • Side: left (backhand).
  • Main partners: Tino Libaak, with whom he formed the "Megapibes", until August 2024; then Alex Chozas; in 2025 Pablo Cardona and Martín Di Nenno; since November 2025 Juan Lebrón.
  • Key results: won the 2023 CMB Monaco Master (P1) with Alex Chozas and Madrid P1 2025 with Martín Di Nenno; at 14 he was called up to Argentina's under-16 national team.

Source: Padel 1969, Tuttosport, Padel Alto, Padel Addict, Wikipedia.

Augsburger in 2026

In 2026 Augsburger and Lebrón won the Brussels P2. At the end of October StudyPadel lists him as number 6 in the FIP ranking.

Sources: Wikipedia – Premier Padel 2026, StudyPadel. Rankings and titles change week by week.

Which racket does Augsburger use

Augsburger is a Siux player and in 2026 his signature racket is the Siux Fenix Pro 2026, sold by shops as the "Siux Leo Augsburger Fenix Pro". It is a diamond with a high balance, an EVA Hard core and a 12K carbon face, with a hard feel; PadelSpeed describes it as built for aggressive, powerful play. For 2027 there is the Siux Fenix Pro 2027, with a 3K SC Power carbon face and 3D texture, at a list price of €359.95: PadelPROShop presents it as the most powerful racket in the Siux 2027 collection. Independent reviews of both are still few. Before Siux he was a Nox player: in 2024 the brand dedicated the LA10 Future to him.

Sources on the model used: Racket Central, Tienda Padel Point, Nox.

Is Augsburger's racket right for you?

  • The Fenix Pro if you are an advanced, attacking player who finishes many points with the smash and wants a powerful, stiff diamond.
  • The 2027 version if you want the new 3K carbon face and are willing to wait for the first independent reviews.
  • None of his tournament rackets if you are a beginner or have elbow problems: look at rackets for beginners or those for tennis elbow.
