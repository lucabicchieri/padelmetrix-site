Beginners often play with a borrowed racket and running shoes. It's the right moment to give them the basic kit: here's what to choose, without overspending.

The first racket

For beginners, the best shape is round: the sweet spot is larger and forgives off-centre hits. A moderate weight and price are better, because in a year they may want to upgrade. Here are some beginner rackets available on Amazon, with the full PadelMetrix page:
