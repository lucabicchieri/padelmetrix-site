An adult racket is too heavy for a child: it tires them out and teaches bad habits. The most useful gift for a young player is a junior racket of the right size; then come a backpack, balls and colourful accessories.

Junior rackets

Junior rackets are shorter and lighter. The right size depends on age and height: if in doubt, ask the club coach. Before buying, check the manufacturer's page for the intended age.
