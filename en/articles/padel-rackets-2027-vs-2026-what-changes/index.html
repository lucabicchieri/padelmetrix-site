Padel rackets 2027 vs 2026: what changes, model by model

Published on 31 October 2026 · by Luca Bicchieri, PadelMetrix

We compared, item by item, the 19 rackets that exist in the PadelMetrix database in both a 2026 and a 2027 version (Bullpadel, Nox, Siux, StarVie). For each model you can see what changes, what stays the same and what cannot be compared because the manufacturer or retailers do not state it.

In short: the shape changes in 5 models (Bullpadel Vertex 05 Geo (Teardrop → Diamond); Nox Ventus Hybrid 12K Lite (Round → Teardrop); Siux Diablo Pro, 2026 sources disagree (Hybrid → Teardrop); Siux Pegasus Pro (Hybrid → Teardrop); StarVie Black Titan (Diamond → Hybrid)). The list price is stated for both versions in only 2 cases (Bullpadel Neuron 02 Edge (€319.99 → €339.99); Siux Electra Pro (€349.99 → €359.95)), always going up. Many 2026 figures were never published, so for several models it is impossible to say whether the 2027 is really different.

Frequently asked questions

Are 2027 rackets very different from the 2026 ones?

It depends on the model. Across 19 updates compared, the shape changes in 5 cases; in the others the stated differences are mostly about face materials, finish and price.

Is the discounted 2026 worth buying?

If shape, weight and balance stay the same and the 2026 is discounted, often yes. If the shape changes, the racket behaves differently and it is worth trying both.
