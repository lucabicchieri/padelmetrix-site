Metalbone Team Light 2026: reviews and opinions

PadelZoom gives it 8.2/10 overall, but without a written review: for now there are only the scores. The highest scores are control (8.5) and ball output (8.5), the lowest is power (7). It is a control racket's profile, consistent with the round shape.

Strengths

  • Good control and precision (8.5/10 according to PadelZoom)
  • High ball output: helps in defence (8.5/10 according to PadelZoom)

Limitations

  • Little power for those who want to finish with the smash (7/10)

The Metalbone Team Light 2026 is right for you if

  • You are moving from beginner to intermediate level
  • You want comfort without spending too much
  • You build the point with patience and precision

Better choose another racket if

  • You are advanced: look at the Carbon versions or the top of the range
  • You mainly want power: a diamond is better
