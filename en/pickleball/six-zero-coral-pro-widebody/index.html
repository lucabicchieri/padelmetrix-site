Coral Pro Widebody: reviews and opinions

In Pickleball Effect's data it has the highest twist weight in the series (7.26) and the lowest swing weight (108): it is very forgiving on off-center hits and moves quickly at the net. Spin stays very high (2,079 rpm). In exchange it has less reach and power than the hybrid and elongated versions. Pickleball Effect lists it as UPA-A approved only: it is not legal in USA Pickleball sanctioned play.

Strengths

  • The most forgiving in the series
  • Quick at the net
  • Very high spin

Limitations

  • UPA-A approval only
  • Less reach and power

It suits you if

  • You want control and margin for error
  • You play a lot at the net

Better choose another paddle if

  • You play USA Pickleball tournaments
  • You want power and reach
