Boom Pro EX15: reviews and opinions

HEAD aims it at aggressive players who attack: the elongated shape gives more power and a higher sweet spot, the midweight makes it suitable all over the court and the long handle suits two-handed shots; the hexagon carbon structure adds stiffness and stability. We have not yet found independent tests with measurements or scores.

Strengths

  • Built for power
  • Midweight, quicker than the Radicals
  • Long handle

Limitations

  • Less suited to the soft game
  • High price
  • No independent tests

It suits you if

  • You play aggressively and want power
  • You use a two-handed backhand

Better choose another paddle if

  • You rely on control and placement: the Gravity is better
  • You are a beginner
