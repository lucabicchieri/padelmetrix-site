Padel racket for tennis elbow

With tennis elbow, choose a soft-feeling racket of 350–365 g, round or teardrop, with a low or medium balance, which transmits less vibration to the arm. A racket won't cure your elbow, but it can avoid making it worse. This page is not a substitute for the advice of a doctor or physiotherapist.

Updated on 24 October 2026 · by Luca Bicchieri, PadelMetrix

What to look for on the spec page

  • Feel: soft or medium-soft. Soft EVA rubbers and fiberglass faces absorb more vibration.
  • Shape: round or teardrop; diamonds concentrate the weight in the head.
  • Weight and balance: 350–365 g, low or medium balance.
  • Comfort and Soft versions: many brands offer them alongside the stiff model, with the same moulds but softer materials.

Frequently asked questions

Which padel racket should you choose with tennis elbow?

In general, rackets with a soft feel, not too heavy and with a low or medium balance are recommended, as they transmit less vibration to the arm. The choice should still be made with your doctor or physiotherapist.

Are diamond rackets bad for the elbow?

Stiff, head-heavy diamonds put more strain on the forearm. There are softer Comfort or Soft versions, but if you are in pain it is better to start with round or teardrop rackets.

Does weight matter for the elbow?

A racket that is too light vibrates more, one that is too heavy tires you: usually it is best to stay in the middle range, around 350–365 grams.
